Vote by census decile

Statistical areas ranked by a 2022 census figure and cut into ten groups, each holding about a tenth of the eligible voters. Shares are summed from box votes, as a percentage of valid votes.

These compare areas, not people: the top wage decile is a set of areas whose median wage is high, and its vote includes everyone who votes there. Boxes are placed by their polling building, which is not always where their voters live. Jewish areas cover 97.7% (2022) and 97.5% (2026) of eligible voters in Jewish, Jerusalem and mixed localities; Arab areas (Muslim or Christian main religion) only 68.3% and 75.1% of those in Arab and Bedouin localities, because about a fifth to a quarter of their boxes could not be placed in an area and 25 of 44 southern Bedouin localities (mostly tribes) have no census figures. In Jewish areas the lowest wage and degree deciles are largely Haredi areas. Druze areas are in neither set. Turnout counts double-envelope voters (mostly soldiers) as not voting.

Vote transfers

Estimated share of each list's voters in the earlier election (rows) who voted for each list, or did not vote, in the later one (columns). Each row sums to 100%.

Elections

% of each row's voters

An ecological estimate from box totals, not a survey: it assumes that a list's voters split the same way in every box, which is not true (Likud voters in Haredi and in secular neighbourhoods do not move alike), and it sees only net flows. It uses only comparable boxes (same number and building, stable size): 43% of eligible voters for 2021 to 2022, 31% for 2022 to 2026, so new neighbourhoods are missing. The intervals reflect sampling between towns only, not the error from that assumption, so the true uncertainty is larger. Small rows (Other lists, Jewish Home) are unstable. Flows to and from "Did not vote" also absorb people who moved, died, came of age or voted by double envelope.

Method

  • Deciles: census 2022 statistical areas (combined areas as the census publishes them; small localities as one unit); median yearly wage of employees, share aged 15+ with an academic degree, share aged 0 to 19. Units are sorted and each goes to the decile holding the midpoint of its eligible voters, recomputed for each election. Main religion from the census. After numbersguys.com.
  • Transfers: comparable boxes as on numbersguys.com: same locality and box number, same building (same venue and address, or within 150 m), eligible voters within 10% of the town's own growth, and, outside Arab, Bedouin and Druze localities, an Arab-party share that moved by less than 25 points. Constrained least squares weighted by eligible voters, rows non-negative and summing to 1; lists under 1% nationally and invalid votes are grouped. 200 bootstrap fits resampling whole localities.
  • Blocs: Bibi bloc and Change bloc as on the map (tables/parties.csv); "Other" adds uncommitted and small lists. Code: scripts/analysis_deciles.py, scripts/analysis_transfers.py. Data: deciles 2022, deciles 2026, transfers 2021 to 2022, transfers 2022 to 2026.
