  1. Central Elections Committee results by polling station, summed by CBS locality code and then by sector. Each locality keeps one fixed membership (2024 codes) in every year.
  2. Double envelopes (soldiers, hospitals, prisons, diplomats) are in no row, Israel included: 2013 5.6%, 2015 5.5%, Apr 2019 5.5%, Sep 2019 6.3%, 2020 7.1%, 2021 9.6%, 2022 9.6%, 2026 7.9% of valid votes. Turnout in every row is therefore a little low, and the Israel row several points low.
  3. Sides follow the site's blocs (tables/parties.csv), seat-winning lists only; votes for lists below the threshold count as other. That is why religious kibbutzim, for example, dip on the right side in April 2019 (New Right) and 2022 (Jewish Home). Kulanu (2015) and Yisrael Beytenu (September 2019) were uncommitted.
  4. Haredi towns: our rule. The census's most common household religiosity is Haredi and UTJ plus Shas won over 50% in 2022 (17 places; Safed, Netivot, Giv'at Ze'ev and others are left out). Northern Bedouin: our list of 25 localities. Settlement form (kibbutz, moshav, community settlement) is from the CBS file and includes West Bank localities.
  5. Locality codes missing from the sector tables were skipped: at most 0.01% of votes in any year. 2026 is not final until Reshumot publication. Hover a cell for its three leading lists. Trend lines use each row's own scale, at least 20 points tall.
